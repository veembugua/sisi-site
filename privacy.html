Privacy notice

At Sisi, we respect your privacy and comply with the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019.

What we collect

We only collect your name, phone number, and ward. We do this solely to send you relevant public participation notices for your specific area in Kiambu County.

How we use it

Your data is never sold. It is used exclusively to keep you informed about county budgets, forums, and voting opportunities in your ward.
