What Sisi does

Sisi is Swahili for us. It is a public participation platform for Kenya's 47 counties, piloting in Kiambu since September 2026.

Register once in your county and ward. After that you receive every county notice, draft bill and budget hearing that affects your ward, and you can respond online instead of travelling to a hall on a working day.

Participation window

Comments on the draft county budget close on 30 September 2026.

Who can take part

Any resident registered in a Kenyan county can participate in local decisions.

You must be 18 years or older and have a valid phone number for verification.

We focus on key documents like the CIDP and the CBEF.
